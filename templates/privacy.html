OUR COMMITMENT

Privacy & responsible access

Cybercrime reporting depends on trust. These principles guide how CECMS handles information.

◇01 · FAIRNESS

No automatic conclusions

Automated classifications, risk indicators, and repeated-contact matches are investigative leads only. They are not proof of guilt.

⌑02 · ACCESS

Role-based visibility

Authorized users can review their own reports. Investigators can access sensitive report details to support case review.

◉03 · MINIMIZATION

Protect personal details

Reporter names, contact information, and evidence notes are restricted to authorized investigators.

✓04 · HUMAN REVIEW

People verify alerts

Investigators must confirm repeated-contact alerts before case status can be changed.

◷05 · ACCOUNTABILITY

Actions are recorded

Authentication, report access, and important case actions are recorded in the security audit trail.

＋06 · SECURITY

Multi-factor protection

Accounts use securely hashed passwords and authenticator verification. Repeated failed logins trigger temporary lockouts.

i

Report safely. Never submit passwords, one-time codes, or complete payment-card details as evidence.
